## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, scholarly environment tailored for higher education. Rejecting the sterile, transactional conventions of enterprise software, it pairs an editorial tradition with contemporary academic workflows. The aesthetic honors physical libraries, archival paper, and classical university typography while providing effortless utility for modern digital coursework.

The visual style blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Nuances**:
- **Dignified & Scholarly:** Governed by deep forest green and tactile paper cream, invoking archival permanence, quiet study halls, and intellectual clarity.
- **Thoughtful & Unhurried:** Generous whitespace, disciplined text tracking, and delicate organic borders eliminate cognitive friction and exam-induced panic.
- **Vibrant Urgency:** Accents of burnt terracotta spotlight active deadlines, critical course milestones, and high-impact actions without breaking visual serenity.
- **Humanist Interface:** Combines authoritative bookish serifs for course narratives and headers with clear, contemporary sans-serif geometry for operational density and metadata readability.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the physical materials of academic life: heavy bound book covers, warm unbleached parchment, and terracotta terracotta brickwork.

### Role Allocation
- **Primary (`#1A3C2E` - Forest Academic Green):** Represents scholarly authority and structure. Applied to primary navigational anchors, persistent brand framing, key focal elements, and authoritative buttons.
- **Secondary (`#D4621A` - Terracotta Orange):** The active accent. Applied selectively to primary calls to action, urgent submission warnings, deadline indicators, and progress highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#EDE6DA` - Muted Linen):** Used for subtle container fills, secondary interactive card states, selected row highlights, and soft divider elements.
- **Neutral Dark (`#11221B` - Deep Ink):** High-contrast, near-black ink tone ensuring WCAG AAA legibility across body text, titles, and high-contrast symbols on paper surfaces.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F5F0E8` - Warm Paper):** The foundational ambient canvas, softening screen glare during multi-hour reading sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Archival White):** Crisp warm white card structures providing layer distinction atop the paper base.
- **Metadata & Subdued Text (`#5C6E64` - Sage Slate):** Mid-tone neutral for timestamps, course codes, author citations, and muted icons.

### Semantic Tones
- **Success (`#2E7D32`):** Olive emerald for graded submissions, completed modules, and passing grades.
- **Warning (`#D97706`):** Warm amber for pending reviews and approaching assignment thresholds.
- **Critical / Urgent (`#C2410C`):** Deep burnt rust for overdue requirements, missing assignments, and immediate alerts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a deliberate duality: **Newsreader** delivers literary distinction, syllabus storytelling, and monumental section leads, while **Plus Jakarta Sans** controls operational components, metrics, navigational labels, and dense coursework tables.

### Rules of Engagement
- **Italic Inflections:** In headlines (`headline-xl` through `headline-md`), intentional italicizing of single contextual keywords (e.g., *Syllabus*, *Thesis*, *Module*) within Newsreader is encouraged to reinforce traditional collegiate publishing.
- **Numerals & Metadata:** Assignment deadlines, grade percentages, timestamps, and course codes (e.g., `HIST 302`) must consistently render in Plus Jakarta Sans with tabular figures where applicable to maintain alignment in course tables and gradebooks.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy (`body-lg`) used in long-form lecture materials or essay readers must never exceed a maximum line width of 68 characters to preserve sustained reading focus.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an asymmetric editorial grid that balances operational utility with spacious reading environments.

### Canvas & Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fixed 280px left navigation rail anchored in deep paper tone, accompanied by an adaptive 12-column editorial canvas. The main reading/workspace area conforms to an asymmetrical split: an 8-column primary content track paired with a 4-column contextual study rail (syllabi, upcoming milestones, annotated footnotes).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Collapsible sidebar menu; grid shifts to 8 columns with 24px gutters. Secondary side rails fold beneath primary module activities.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column vertical stream governed by 20px outer safe margins. Navigation transitions to a persistent, compact bottom rail paired with a streamlined top app bar showing current module progress.

### Spacing Principles
Whitespace acts as a functional buffer against cognitive fatigue. Component vertical rhythm adheres strictly to the defined spacing intervals:
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for inline metadata pairing, badge interiors, and dense table cell groupings.
- Use `space-md` for standard card padding and form control separation.
- Use `space-lg` and `space-xl` to segment primary thematic units, essay sections, and lecture overview modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile surface layering and ambient illumination rather than artificial volumetric drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Character
Shadows are ultra-diffused, tinted with the primary dark forest pigment (`rgba(26, 60, 46, 0.05)` to `0.08`), grounding cards naturally into the warm paper background:
- **Resting Surface (Level 1):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 60, 46, 0.05);` Provides separation between crisp white content containers and the `#F5F0E8` canvas.
- **Interactive Hover (Level 2):** `box-shadow: 0 8px 28px -4px rgba(26, 60, 46, 0.09); transform: translateY(-1px);` Applied to selectable assignments, courses, and interactive schedule cards.
- **Overlay & Modals (Level 3):** `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(26, 60, 46, 0.16);` Used for submission drawers, grading rubrics, and modal dialogs, coupled with an ambient backdrop overlay (`rgba(17, 34, 27, 0.40)` with 4px backdrop blur).

### Outlines & Borders
Cards and elevated panels combine depth with fine 1px structural outlines:
- Surface containers leverage `border: 1px solid rgba(227, 218, 205, 0.8)` or deep forest translucency `border: 1px solid rgba(26, 60, 46, 0.08)`. This creates a crisp, bookbinder's edge across all surfaces.

## Shapes

The interface embraces tailored, organic geometry. Structural elements rely on balanced rounded corners (level 2), pairing crisp geometric legibility with an approachable hand-crafted warmth.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Standard Controls & Cards:** Small inputs, search bars, and course modules utilize `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- **Major Panels & Cards:** Course overview containers, lecture modals, and grade cards apply `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for architectural containment.
- **Badges & Expressive Controls:** Status markers, topic tags, pill buttons, and filter toggles adopt full capsule pill curvature (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular text blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Scholarly Action:** Background `#1A3C2E`, text `#F5F0E8`, font weight 600 in `label-lg`, border-radius 16px (`rounded-lg`). Hover shifts to `#234D3C` with Level 2 elevation.
- **Accent Deadline Action:** Background `#D4621A` (Terracotta), text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius 16px. Used exclusively for "Submit Assignment", "Enroll", and high-priority milestones.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with 1px border `#1A3C2E25`, text `#1A3C2E`. On hover: background `#EDE6DA`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding 4px 12px, font size 12px (`label-md`).
- **Academic Tags:** Background `#EDE6DA`, text `#1A3C2E`.
- **Urgent Milestones:** Background `rgba(212, 98, 26, 0.12)`, text `#D4621A`, 1px border `rgba(212, 98, 26, 0.25)`.
- **Completed / Graded:** Background `rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.12)`, text `#2E7D32`, 1px border `rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.25)`.

### Cards
- **Course & Module Cards:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `rgba(227, 218, 205, 0.9)`, border-radius 16px, Level 1 shadow. Cards feature an inner padding of `space-lg` (24px).
- **Featured Lecture Cards:** Feature an editorial top border accent in `#1A3C2E` (3px thickness) and an optional subtle cream tint header.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Standard Text Fields:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `rgba(227, 218, 205, 1.0)`, border-radius 12px, text `#11221B`.
- **Focus State:** 2px ring `#1A3C2E` with 0px offset, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Search Rails:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) background `#EDE6DA` with placeholder color `#5C6E64`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 20px box with 6px border-radius (`rounded-sm`), border 1.5px solid `#5C6E64`. Checked state fills `#1A3C2E` with white checkmark glyph.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric circles, active outer ring `#1A3C2E`, inner circle dot `#1A3C2E`.

### Academic-Specific Components
- **Assignment Milestone Tracker:** A clean horizontal progress bar using a 6px rail in `#EDE6DA`, filled with terracotta `#D4621A` for active progress and forest `#1A3C2E` upon submission.
- **Reading Dossier / Lecture View:** Dedicated distraction-free layout containing Newsreader typography, margin notes aligned on the right study rail, and a pinned progress indicator at the top edge.
- **Grade Rubric Matrix:** Warm white surface divided by hairline `#EDE6DA` borders, utilizing Plus Jakarta Sans tabular figures for point allocations and criteria weights.
